ok and the green chip mean exactly valid. A layer-1 200 proves auth, not balance: only OpenRouter GET /key with credit, a layer-2 ping or a live 200 chat may write valid. OAuth needs_reauth: openprogram providers login <pid>. A non-JSON 200 from a custom endpoint is not valid.

Results are cached 60 s per provider and whether a model was named; use_cache=False or refresh=True bypasses. The secret is never stored or returned. Not a quota dashboard.

Adding a provider

Declare its kind in _kind_for; openai_bearer needs nothing. Anthropic-wire third parties need three things to agree, pinned by test_model_fetch_routing.py:

  • _kind_for returns anthropic_compat
  • catalog/metadata.py default_api_for resolves anthropic-messages
  • listing/fetchers/__init__.py routes to the base-URL-aware Anthropic fetcher

Probe log

models/listing/probe_log.py marks test_provider (connectivity), validate_credential (credential) and fetch_and_normalize (model_list). Requests through safe_http.observing_requests become probe_events rows: provider, kind, method, origin, path (never the query), status or error type, duration. Answered model lists are skipped; a nested probe takes the outer kind. Never counted as usage. Kept 30 days; GET /api/providers/{name}/probes?days=7, newest first. Follows magpie's probelog (yetone/magpie, MIT).
