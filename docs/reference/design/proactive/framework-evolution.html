A new consumer plugs in with one subscribe(types=…) instead of integrating five or six mechanisms, some moments of which had no signal at all.

  • tool.before is the tool interception point. It sits outside the approval wrapper and applies to subagents. turn.stop is the other gate.
  • A bridge is the pattern for a subsystem that already models its events well: nothing inside auth knows the bus exists.
  • Connecting a source is additive: the bus gets a copy and the old path keeps running, so each source is verifiable and reversible alone.
  • Switching the WebUI uses a pass-through envelope: broadcast frames are byte-for-byte the same, so no shadow comparison is needed and the frontend does not notice.
