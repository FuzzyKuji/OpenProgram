Width behavior

components/layout/use-resizable-rail.ts is the only implementation: saves expanded widths, enforces limits, handles pointer capture, generates expanded and collapsed styles, controls transitions. Each sidebar passes its limits and growth direction.

AspectRule
Handle.rail-resize-handle, hit area about 8px either side of the divider, no effect on layout width. The shell permits its overflow; the inner content stays clipped.
DragPrimary button only; touch panning and text selection on the handle disabled. Hovering shows the same short grip as a split's divider, centred in the 8px gap between the cards; dragging makes it solid, and it turns the accent colour while the width rests on the default.
Back to the defaultEither rail: a double-click on the handle, or Enter with the handle focused, returns to 288px over 280ms, easing out. The width is walked a frame at a time, as in a drag, so what is inside the rail moves with its edge. A drag within 8px of 288px lands on it. At the default nothing is stored.
Drag endsRelease, pointer cancel or window blur. Window-level end events back up the captured handle events; a move that sees a released button also ends it. The collapse animation is restored each time.

Opening and closing

Every part exists in both states: opening and closing never mount, unmount or change display inside the rail, in either direction or when toggled mid-move. The shell's width starts in the click's frame; .collapsed changes in that render. Rules: app/styles/base/layout.css, "Opening and closing".

Why no inherited property changes

A move changes no inherited property (pointer-events, visibility, color, custom properties). The browser restyles the whole subtree under such a change, which in the click's frame is the whole conversation list: 1,764 elements for pointer-events, 1,946 for visibility, 1 for opacity (traced).

use-resizable-rail.ts writes inert on the outermost open-only parts itself at the settle (the width's transitionend, or a timer), so that restyle lands after the move. A part that mounts while the rail is at rest collapsed becomes inert with it.

Settled state and first paint
  • The toggle's two glyphs (hide, show) stay mounted and cross-fade.
  • data-rail-closed marks a settled close. Only what cannot be seen keys on it: the narrow layout's return to the flow beside a split canvas, the left list's scroll reset, paused running markers in the hidden list.
  • A left sidebar closed while scrolled slides its links to the top with the width (one Web Animation on transform) and resets the scroll at the settle in the same frame.
  • The right panel's views work only while it is open: Activity, Resources' processes, page pictures and terminal reads pause while collapsed.
  • A stored closed left sidebar paints collapsed in the first frame under html[data-sidebar-closed], no transition, its open-only parts out of reach by interactivity: inert until the mounted rail makes them inert.

Acceptance
  • Drag each sidebar to its limit and collapse: it becomes 62px within 200ms, nothing vanishing or appearing in one frame.
  • Expanding restores the previous width.
  • Double-clicking a rail's handle returns it to 288px, and it stays there after a reload.
  • Dragging either rail with a long conversation open shows no scrollbar in the chat.
  • Dragging can begin within about 8px either side of the divider, shows the short grip, and continues after the pointer leaves the handle.
