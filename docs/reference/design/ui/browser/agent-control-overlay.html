Look and motion

ElementSpecification
Glow shape18pt accent stroke along the rounded edge (10pt corners), blurred 11pt, 55% opacity, clipped to the window. No line on the edge, nothing outside.
Glow breathing60% to 100% of that strength every 2.6 s
Covered partsWindows in front are cut out of glow and lights
LightsThree accent circles over close, minimize, zoom, half a point larger. A white layer rises to 40% and falls every 1.4 s, 0.2 s apart. No buttons, no lights. Clicks reach the button below.
Cursor glyphSolar cursor, bold duotone, 26px, accent, tail at half strength, on a white keyline from Solar cursor-bold showing 2px, plus a faint contact shadow
Hot spotBox centred on the action point; arrow tip 2px right and below, so the dot shows beside it. Element: centre of its frame before the action. Coordinates: screenshot point mapped to screen.
StayingOver a desktop app it stays where the last step left it and moves with the window; first step starts mid-window. Over a held page it stays between steps; a step without a hold fades after 2.8 s.
TravelGentle arc, bend 16% of distance up to 56pt, ease-in-out, 240–680 ms by distance
PressClick, press, selection, drag or open: arrow dips to 82% toward its tip and springs back in 0.26 s; an 8px accent dot with 2px white ring settles and fades within 1 s. Typing and scrolling neither press nor leave a dot.
Reduced motionCursor jumps; press, dot, breathing and pulsing off

Accent colour

--agent-accent is --theme-accent (themes/fallback.css), used by glow, lights, cursor and click dot; keyline, dot ring and light pulse are white. The overlay shares the App's origin and theme settings, so a change reaches it at once.

Safety

RuleDetail
No inputIgnores the mouse, never takes focus or activates. Nothing moves the system pointer.
Visible in controlUp for the whole window session, fades in 0.36 s. A minimized window or another Space hides it until back.
Per conversationEach conversation shows its own
Display onlyFailures are dropped; the step goes on
No Stop controlA clickable area would catch clicks meant for the app. Stopping is the conversation's stop control and the Running panel. The webpage floating control offers only Review request while confirmation waits.

Implementation status

Implemented on macOS and the three web surfaces. The macOS live-capture marker (a badge over the title-bar buttons, the menu-bar icon) is still requested but does not show in screenshots, so the accent lights mark the buttons.
