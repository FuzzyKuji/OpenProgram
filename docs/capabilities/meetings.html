Meetings

Record a call you are in, in Zoom, Teams, a browser or any app, and get a transcript and a summary with decisions, action items and open questions.

Record a meeting

  1. Open Meetings from its new tab tile, or Open on the Applications page.
  2. Optional: name the meeting and pick the spoken language (Detect automatically by default, last choice remembered). Press Start recording.
  3. First time: approve Microphone, asked by the window, and Screen & System Audio Recording, asked by macOS for the OpenProgram App. Nothing on the screen is recorded.
  4. The window shows elapsed time and pieces uploaded and transcribed. Other tabs and windows are fine.
  5. Press Stop recording. The last pieces are transcribed, the summary is written, and it appears under Past meetings.

Recording never starts on its own. Without headphones your microphone hears the other side; lines repeating the computer's sound are dropped, but headphones give the cleanest transcript. The Settings → Voice language applies to voice input only. Without a chosen language each piece is detected on its own, and the card and Past meetings show the choice, or "detected".

Storage

~/.openprogram/meetings/<id>/
  meta.json          name, start, length, progress, summary chat, whether
                     computer sound was recorded and speakers told apart
  tracks/mic/…       microphone, 30-second WebM pieces
  tracks/system/…    computer's sound, 30-second WAV pieces
  transcript.json    merged transcript
  summary.md         summary, full transcript at the end

Under the state directory, ~/.openprogram by default. OpenAI or Groq: each piece goes to that service. whisper.cpp: audio stays here. Telling speakers apart always runs here.

Limits

  • One meeting at a time, at most 4 hours; it stops itself at the limit.
  • Speakers are separated only after stopping. Similar voices can share a number; one person can get two when their sound changes a lot.
  • Silent pieces are skipped. A piece that cannot be converted or transcribed is reported under the transcript; the rest goes on.

Turn Meetings off

Its switch on the Applications page, or openprogram programs disable meeting, which stops a recording in progress. openprogram programs enable meeting turns it back on. See Applications.
